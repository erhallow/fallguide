Where to look

  • Southern & rural · go Oct 5–18Lowell, DeMotte, Rensselaer, Wheatfield, Knox, North Judson: first to turn
  • Inland corridor · go Oct 8–21Valparaiso, LaPorte, Crown Point
  • Lakeshore & Dunes · go Oct 15–28Michigan City, Porter, Chesterton. The lake keeps nights warm, so color comes late and lingers.
