# Fall Guide — extra link review

Review snapshot: September 23, 2026. Reviewed the current [live guide](https://fallguide.nwiexplored.com/) and the matching repository version at `fab6ad0`.

**Approval follow-up:** The recommended changes have since been implemented and verified. See the [implementation summary](./implementation.md) for decisions, checks and subsequent approved updates. This report and its link-by-link inventory preserve the pre-edit research snapshot.

## Bottom line

The guide has many good, specific links, but several still make readers search a homepage, Facebook feed or general calendar. A few links open without providing usable event information. There are also some concrete copy corrections and conflicting organizer details worth addressing before promoting the guide more widely.

**Every one of the 186 link occurrences is documented:** 159 unique web destinations, repeated in 179 outbound links, plus six internal links and one phone link. This is a completed audit, not a claim that every destination or 2026 detail could be verified. Inaccessible and inconclusive sources are explicitly marked.

Open the [complete link-by-link audit](./link-by-link.md) for each destination's relevance, accuracy, ease of finding information, recommendation and any reviewed alternative. The index follows the guide's link order; its IDs are new audit IDs, not the IDs from the earlier review.

**At the time of this audit, no website edits, commits or pushes were made.** Only local audit documents, review data and audit helper scripts were added. See the approval follow-up above for subsequent local implementation.

## 5. Town-hours section: restructure for trust

Of the **31 municipalities**, Knox is the only one whose linked source was verified here as confirming 2026 neighborhood Halloween hours. Most other destinations are appropriate official sites, but they are not evidence for the adjacent time. Westville has a promising indexed announcement that could not be safely opened; that is not enough to mark it confirmed.

I recommend showing **confirmed 2026 hours only** in the main table. Put the rest under “Not announced/verified yet” with an accurately labeled official-site or phone fallback. Remove the 2025 hours, or tuck them into a clearly separate historical reference with their actual sources. Even labeled historical times are easy to misread while scanning on a phone.

Do not substitute municipal trunk-or-treat, downtown-business trick-or-treat or zoo events for Halloween neighborhood hours. The full audit records each town separately and flags La Porte's inaccessible government link.

## 6. Reader-friendly linking policy

For a local-newsletter lead magnet, the guide should save a planning step:

1. Put **date, time, city/venue, price and key restriction** in the card itself where verified.
2. Label the destination honestly: “Event details,” “Tickets,” “Facebook event,” “Official town site,” or “Event flyer (PDF).” Avoid sending attendees to an unexplained vendor application.
3. Prefer a current organizer event page, then a venue/municipal event listing, then an identified secondary community calendar. A specific URL is not automatically better if its year is wrong or it does not load.
4. Keep “source reviewed” separate from “2026 date confirmed.” A working link and a current footer do not prove the event details.
5. Normal scrolling within the correct event page is acceptable. Searching a feed or calendar for the event is the friction to remove.
6. Where no reliable dated source can be found, keep the item explicitly unconfirmed or temporarily omit it from the dated list. Do not imply it is canceled.

The updated **Aperion Care Arbors** link passed this recheck and does not need removal. Many official festival pages, direct Facebook events, NPS trail pages, newsletter links and internal navigation also passed. The guide's nine existing static checks passed; those tests verify structure, not external event accuracy.
