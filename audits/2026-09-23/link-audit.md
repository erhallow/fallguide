## Coverage and how to read this inventory

All **138 anchor occurrences** in the live guide are listed below, including repeated destinations: **135 external occurrences, 125 unique external destinations, and 3 internal navigation links**. The live HTML and local `index.html` were byte-identical at the audit snapshot. IDs refer to occurrences in source order, not an event's permanent identifier. Source line references apply to that snapshot; the HTML has many listings compressed onto a single line.

Each external destination received a retrieval attempt and content review. Browser checks and official-source retrieval were used to investigate blocked, dynamic, or ambiguous pages; the two linked DNR PDFs were also visually inspected. A successful HTTP response was not treated as proof of relevance. Facebook and Instagram previews sometimes exposed only the event name/date, not a full event description. Those limits are recorded rather than presented as a full verification. No account login, purchase, registration, or organizer contact was performed.

**Match** means the destination is relevant; it does not certify every date, price, restriction, or promotional claim. Read the note even on matching links. **Wrong destination** means a different event/entity. **Wrong edition** means a previous year's page. **Content conflict** means the guide conflicts with its relevant source. **Source conflict** means source evidence is stale or inconsistent. **Weak support** means an organization/profile page does not establish the claimed event. **Limited verification** means some public evidence matched but full details could not be checked. **Unverified** means meaningful public verification was unavailable.
